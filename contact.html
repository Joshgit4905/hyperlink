Questions, answered.

Do you take reservations?

Seating is first come, first served. For groups of eight or more, send us a note and we'll save you the long table.

Are your pastries vegan or gluten-free?

We bake one vegan pastry and one gluten-free pastry every day. Our kitchen handles nuts, dairy and gluten.

Can I buy beans for my office?

Yes. Wholesale orders start at 5 kg per month and come with a free grinder calibration visit.

Is there parking?

There is two-hour street parking on Roast Street and covered bike racks next to the patio.

Prices include tax. Oat, almond and whole milk available at no extra cost. Please let our baristas know about any allergies before ordering.
